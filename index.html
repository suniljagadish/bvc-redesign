04 · Mission + vision

What
drives us

To be one of Canada's leading indoor cycling communities and centre of excellence, where every rider can discover their potential and pursue greatness.

The Burnaby Velodrome Club unites riders through the sport of track cycling. We offer accessible programs, meaningful development pathways, competitive racing, and strong partnerships, in a welcoming, safe environment for everyone, from first-time riders to world-class athletes.

01

Inclusiveness

Riders of all ages, abilities, and backgrounds. We work to remove barriers to the sport.

02

Excellence

High performance and continuous improvement, on and off the track.

03

Community

A connected cycling culture where people belong and support one another.

04

Development

Clear pathways for every rider, from grassroots to elite competition.

05

Partnership

Working with athletes, families, clubs, and organizations to strengthen the sport.
