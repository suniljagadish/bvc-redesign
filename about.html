01 · Our story

Built by
volunteers

Racing has been part of the club since the very beginning. Before Burnaby there was China Creek. When that track came down, the community built a new one.

  1. 1954

    China Creek Velodrome is built in Vancouver for the Empire Games.

  2. 1980

    China Creek is demolished to make way for VCC's Broadway campus.

  3. 1991–97

    Cycling BC and a crew of volunteers build a new track inside Harry Jerome Sports Centre.

  4. 1997

    The Burnaby Velodrome opens its doors.

  5. 2026

    Two BVC juniors are selected for the Junior World Championships.
