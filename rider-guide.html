01 · Before you come

Your first
visit

Track hours are limited and change monthly, so check the schedule first. Arrive 10 to 15 minutes early for orientation and bike sizing.

Wear
  • Your usual cycling kit, or athletic shorts and a moisture-wicking top
  • No cotton
  • A light jacket. The building can get chilly
Bring
  • Helmet (we can lend one)
  • Water bottle and a snack
  • Clipless pedals and shoes, if you use them
Leave at home
  • Your road bike. Track bikes only on the boards; our rental fleet has every size
  • Headphones and music players

03 · Safety

Different,
not dangerous

Our track is smaller than others in the region, not more dangerous. The goal, from weekly training to international racing, is to keep everyone as safe as possible.

  • Learn to Ride and Learn to Race are mandatory for all first-time riders and racers. No exceptions.
  • Coached workouts follow a skills-based progression.
  • Mind the lip between the Côte d'Azur and the apron.
  • See something unsafe? Tell a club official.

06 · FAQ

Good
questions

Can't find it? Contact us.

Can I ride my road bike on the track?+

No. Only track bikes are allowed, but we have a fleet of rental bikes in every size.

I'm worried I can't go fast enough. Should I still try?+

Yes. Because the banking is steep, the minimum rolling speed is 30 km/h. Most beginners who fall do so from panic, so riding a little faster actually helps. Coaches are there the whole time.

Do I have to race?+

Not at all. Plenty of members just ride. Racing runs October to March, and clinics like Learn to Race are open if you get curious.

What comes after Learn to Ride?+

Join Novice coached workouts. After a few sessions a coach will either suggest more novice sessions or clear you for open track and intermediate workouts.

Can I just come and ride any time?+

Once you've finished Learn to Ride, yes, during scheduled sessions and open track times. At least two people need to be present to ride. Hours change monthly, so check the schedule.

Do I need to bring pedals?+

If you ride clipless, bring yours. Otherwise we'll lend you pedals with toe clips.

Why ride track if I'm a road or mountain biker?+

Track skills transfer to every discipline. You can't coast, so a track session is usually more intense than a road ride of the same length.

Can I come and watch?+

Always. Friday Night Racing runs October through March, and our bigger race weekends are great to watch.

Where is the velodrome?+

Inside the Harry Jerome Sports Centre on the north side of Burnaby Mountain, about 20 minutes' drive east of downtown Vancouver. 7564 Barnet Highway, Burnaby.

How much does it cost?+

Current memberships, passes and fees are all on Uplifter. Go to the Member hub.
