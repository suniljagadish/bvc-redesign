01 · Start here

Learn
to Ride

The first step onto the track. Learn the technique and etiquette to ride the velodrome safely, skills that carry over to most tracks. Open to young adults and older at any ability. If you can ride a bike, you're ready.

For new track ridersMost popular

Learn to Ride

Five 90-minute coached sessions. Book any open date, finish in a week or spread it over months. You'll come out ready to ride the track and join other programs.

5 × 90 min · max 20 riders
For a single experience

Try the Track

One 90-minute coached session. Included for new members with a general membership. Come on your own or bring friends.

1 × 90 min
How to register
  1. 1Buy a Learn to Ride pass on Uplifter.
  2. 2Join BVC on Spond and sign up for your sessions.
  3. 3Arrive 10 to 15 minutes early for orientation, bike sizing and a tour.
Get started →
What you'll learn
  • Riding and handling a fixed-gear bike
  • Entering, exiting and riding the track safely
  • Velodrome etiquette
  • Riding pace lines
  • Riding structured workouts
What's included
  • Five coached sessions with BVC coaches
  • A rental bike
  • A helmet, if you don't have one
  • Insurance
What to bring
  • Helmet, water bottle and a light snack
  • Running shoes, or clipless pedals and shoes (recommended)
  • A UCI licence, if you're from out of province
  • Signed waiver from a parent or guardian, if 17 or under

Passes expire August 31, the end of the membership year. International riders need a UCI licence for any event or workout.

03 · A thrill like no other

Learn
to Race

The theory and practice of mass-start racing. Repeat the sessions as often as you like until you're ready for the winter Friday Night Racing series, then keep learning with coaching after each race.

Before you start
  • Complete all Learn to Ride sessions
  • Attend at least two Novice workouts
When it runs
Fall and winter
Monthly classes. Dates on the schedule.
Summer
Learn to Race Level II, every Friday night.
How to register
  1. 1Log in to Uplifter

  3. 3Book your sessions
